DOT 4 brake fluid joins the Eastern distribution range.

Published · 2026-05-22 · By Kolben Canada · 4 MIN READ · Available in EN · FR

A reformulated DOT 4 brake fluid (SKU KB-420) joins the catalogue across Ontario and Quebec from this week. Kolben Chemicals Canada is adding a reformulated DOT 4 brake fluid (SKU KB-420) to the Eastern distribution range from this week. The new revision targets workshops, fleets, and pro shops carrying the Kolben line in Ontario and Quebec, with bilingual EN/FR labels and updated technical and safety documentation on file. This copy is placeholder pending review.

The reformulation lifts the fluid's dry boiling point and keeps compatibility with modern ABS, ESP, and hydraulic-clutch systems. SDS and TDS revisions are available in the Compliance & SDS library; distributors can request a printed pack from the customer-service desk.

What is in the catalogue

The release is part of the 2026 range update. Distributors carrying Kolben already have catalogue access from this week, with first deliveries timed to the regular monthly cycle. The product specification, application notes, and certifications are documented in the technical data sheet — link the TDS from the compliance library rather than restating values here.

  • Dry boiling point raised in the new revision; see TDS for the confirmed value.
  • ABS, ESP and modern hydraulic-clutch compatibility retained.
  • Bilingual EN/FR labels standard on every Canadian SKU.
  • SDS R-2026.05 available in both languages from the Compliance library.
The point of the 2026 update is procurement clarity — one SKU, one bilingual label, one current SDS, ordered the same way in Quebec or Alberta. — Placeholder attribution · Kolben Canada

How to order

Existing distributors place orders through the standard channel. Shops and fleets without a distributor relationship should use the Where to Buy tool to locate the nearest authorised distributor, or contact the Canadian sales desk for a sample pack.
