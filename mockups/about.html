Who we are

One supplier. The full range.

Canadian importer Bilingual EN · FR Trade-only Full-line catalogue

Kolben sources, supplies and brings to market a full line of professional automotive chemicals — cleaners, additives, lubricants, brake and cooling chemistries — formulated to recognized industry specifications and distributed across Canada through trade partners.

Our role is to make a precision-grade range available to Canadian distributors, jobbers, fleet operators and pro shops as a single, accountable supplier — with bilingual labelling, current safety data sheets, and reliable supply from our YYZ and YEG warehouses.

The Kolben name and design language draw on a German design philosophy — geometric type, sharp corners, restraint over ornament. That philosophy shapes how the brand looks and reads. It does not describe where products are formulated, manufactured or sourced; those facts are documented on each product’s technical and safety data sheet.

Why this matters

Bilingual labels and current SDS are non-negotiable for a Canadian distributor — a missing data sheet or English-only label can pull a SKU from a shelf. We carry this paperwork as a baseline so partners don’t inherit the risk.
