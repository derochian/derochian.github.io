Regulatory context

The Canadian frameworks these documents serve.

A neutral, plain-language overview of the three regulations Kolben products are most often referenced against in Canada. This is context only — the linked Safety Data Sheet is the authoritative source for any hazard, first-aid, classification, or transport detail.

CCCR · 2001
Consumer chemical labelling
The Consumer Chemicals and Containers Regulations, 2001 set the labelling and packaging requirements for chemical products sold to consumers in Canada — pictograms, signal words, first-aid statements, and child-resistant closures where required.
Administered by
Health Canada — Consumer Product Safety
HPR · WHMIS · 2015
Workplace SDS & classification
The Hazardous Products Regulations align with WHMIS 2015 (GHS-aligned) and require a current Safety Data Sheet for hazardous products supplied to a Canadian workplace. Distributors and end-use workplaces keep SDS on file and accessible to workers.
Administered by
Health Canada — WHMIS / Provincial OHS authorities
TDG · SOR/2001-286
Transport of dangerous goods
The Transportation of Dangerous Goods Regulations govern classification, packaging, marking, and shipping documentation for regulated products moved by road, rail, air, or marine across Canada. The SDS section 14 carries the transport classification used for shipping.
Administered by
Transport Canada — TDG Directorate
Authoritative source
The linked Safety Data Sheet is the authoritative source for any hazard, first-aid, classification, exposure, or transport detail. If a value on this page differs from the linked SDS, the SDS prevails.
